About XDLab

XDLab is a space engineering and artificial intelligence research laboratory at Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University. Our work advances computational methods, autonomous systems, space sustainability, mission design, and AI-enabled approaches for understanding and improving the future of space operations.

Research Areas

Our laboratory is positioned at the intersection of space engineering, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and the solid foundations in computation, numerical methods, optimization, dynamics, and control.

Efficient Space Systems

Efficiency is increasingly critical in space system design, operation, and research. We innovate in key areas including Numerical Methods (Koopman Operator), Feature Generations (Proper Elements), Dynamical System (Chaos and Manifold Dynamics), and improve Space Sustainability and enable Space System Design.

Learn More →

Space AI+

Artificial Intelligence built for Space offers substantial potential for all space applications. We innovate in key areas of GAI application in Space, providing PSA (problem set astrodynamics) Benchmark for LLM, develop reinforcement agent exploring space launching policy, building blocks towards a balanced Space AI+.

Learn More →

ASI (Anthropology-Space Interaction)

We innovate in ASI, the study of how humans interact with space, and what are the socio-economical impact of such interaction, and the influence on science and technology systematically.

Learn More →

Space Sustainability & Policy

We connect technical space environment modeling with policy-informed approaches to support long-term orbital sustainability, responsible space operations, space traffic coordination, and decision-making for increasingly complex space systems.

Current Projects

XDLab develops computational, AI-enabled, and policy-aware approaches to improve the safety, sustainability, and efficiency of space systems.

Space Sustainability Modeling

Modeling the long-term evolution of the orbital environment and evaluating strategies for debris mitigation, space traffic coordination, and sustainable space operations.

AI for Astrodynamics

Developing benchmarks, tools, and AI-enabled methods for evaluating large language models and intelligent systems in space engineering and astrodynamics problems.

Mission Design and Optimization

Applying numerical methods, dynamical systems theory, and optimization techniques to improve mission planning, trajectory design, and space system performance.

Space Policy and Operations

Connecting technical modeling with policy development to support decision-making for orbital debris, emerging space systems, responsible operations, and future space governance.
